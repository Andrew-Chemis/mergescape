## 1. What Mergescape is

Mergescape is a single-player idle game. Your character gains experience only from your own real activity on GitHub: opening, reviewing and merging pull requests. A read-only GitHub App tells the service about that activity, and the game shows your character, a Combat Log that explains every score, a weekly run and a shop.

There is nothing to buy. There is no payment of any kind. Gold is earned by shipping and can never be bought, and there are no loot boxes, subscriptions or real-money stores.
