The Moguls · Act III bosses

Six parody archetypes, one per week, each with a modifier that is both a joke and a nudge. Invented names, no real names or logos anywhere. The Bestiary carries the disclaimer: any resemblance to persons living, dead or on a podcast is satire.

Mogul

The Rocket Man

Hardcore week. Deletions count double, night events +1 Power, unmerged PRs cost 5 gold. "The best part is no part."

Mogul

The Leather Jacket

The more you merge, the more you save. Each merge in a row adds +0.1 Power (cap +1.5); an unmerged close resets the chain.

Mogul

The Turtleneck

One more thing. PRs under 100 lines ×3, over 400 ×0.5. An approval with exactly one inline comment is a design review: ×2.

Mogul

The Hoodie

Move fast. Only PRs merged within 24 hours of opening score, at ×2. Everything else goes to the metaverse.

Mogul

The Founder

Non-profit to for-profit. Points ×0.5, gold ×4. Wednesday the Board removes a random Charm; Friday it returns with +0.25 Power.

Mogul

The Two-Pizza Boss

Two-pizza teams. Merges with 2+ human reviewers ×2.5, party bonus ×3, solo merges score 0.

Bestiary · pull requests as monsters

The monster is chosen from the PR's shape. Cosmetic, but it makes the log readable at a glance.

Mob

Merge Goblin

Any ordinary merged PR under 300 lines. Clutches a tiny purple merge icon.

Mob

Nitpick Imp

A review with 5+ inline comments. Hurls quotation marks.

Mob

Flaky Test Wraith

A PR pushed to 4+ times before merge. Flickers red and green.

Mob

Legacy Golem

A merged PR of 1,000+ lines. Carved with COBOL runes. Size points capped at 120.

Elite

Rebase Hydra

Three or more review rounds. Each head is a branch. Drops a pick-1-of-3.

Elite

Scope Creep

Open more than 14 days before merging. Grew out of a ticket.

Elite

Release Dragon

A merge that closes 5+ linked PRs or issues. Curled around a v1.0.0 tag.

Elite

The Silent Monolith

A PR merged with no review of any kind. One unread notification dot.

Elites drop 25 gold and a free Charm pack. Bosses are the Moguls above.

Pledges · Slay the Spire's cards

Draw five on Monday, play up to three. A Pledge is a contract for the week: hit it and collect, miss it and nothing happens. The number is the contract target.

3Pledge

Strike

Merge 3 PRs this week. +300 points on the third.

5Pledge

Defend

Submit 5 scoring reviews. +1 Power on reviews for the rest of the week.

1Pledge

Big Game Hunter

Review a PR of 800+ lines. That review scores ×3.

2Pledge

Mentor

Approve 2 PRs from authors with fewer merges than you. +0.5 Power until Sunday.

0Pledge

Clean Sweep

Close zero PRs unmerged this week. Upgrades the next chest.

3Pledge+

Pair Up

Merge 3 PRs each with a party bonus. +600 points.

All numbers on this page follow the scoring in SPEC.md §4–6: XP = Points × Power × (Level+1)^1.6, levels from the Diablo II experience table. Sprites are 16×16 placeholders drawn to establish silhouette and palette; final art should keep the same proportions.
