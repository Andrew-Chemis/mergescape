# CLAUDE.md

## Baton Board

Track work on the Baton board (project `mergescape`) with the `baton` skill from
the `chemis@skills` plugin: `baton take` a card before editing, `baton pass` it
when done. Record anything worth keeping (a win, friction, a bug) in
the owner's Baton field log; a session that cannot reach it leaves the
note as a `baton comment` on the card, prefixed `[field-log]`.
